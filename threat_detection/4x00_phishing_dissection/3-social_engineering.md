## Email 2 — Portal verification lure

- Psychological lever: Urgency, fear and impersonation.
- Pretext: MedDefense IT Security claims a policy update requires portal re-verification within 24 hours.
- Requested action: Click the link and verify portal access.
- Targeting level: TARGETED.
- Content red flags: 24-hour deadline, account lockout threat, MedDefense impersonation and references to staff systems.
- Attacker knowledge required: Recipient name, MedDefense employment and use of staff systems.
- Conclusion: A targeted credential-phishing lure designed to pressure the recipient into immediate verification.

## Email 3 — Microsoft account lure

- Targeting level: TARGETED.

## Email 5 — Invoice lure

- Psychological lever: Financial pressure and urgency.
- Pretext: MedEquip Supplies claims payment is due for medical supplies delivered to MedDefense.
- Requested action: Pay through the linked portal or open/retrieve the attached invoice.
- Targeting level: TARGETED.
- Content red flags: USD 24,716.38 payment request, 7-day deadline, late-fee threat, payment links and PDF attachment.
- Attacker knowledge required: MedDefense organization, Accounts Payable workflow and recipient's AP role.
- Conclusion: A targeted financial phishing lure designed around an invoice and payment workflow.
