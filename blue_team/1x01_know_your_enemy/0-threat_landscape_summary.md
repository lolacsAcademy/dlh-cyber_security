# Threat Landscape Summary — MedDefense Health Systems

## 1. Threat Actors
- Ransomware/RaaS — money, medium-high skill, buys access, splits profit. #1 threat, matches our size + budget.
- Nation-state — wants research/IP, very high skill. Low risk, no research programs.
- Insider — 60% negligent (lost devices, shared logins), 40% malicious (theft, snooping). High risk — shared radiology login, no offboarding.
- Hacktivist — political targets, low-med skill, mostly DDoS. Low risk, not controversial.
- Unskilled/opportunistic — scans whole internet, not targeted. High risk — already hit us (billing-srv-01 miner).

## 2. Why Healthcare Gets Hit
- Downtime risks patient safety → hospitals pay faster (60% vs 46% avg).
- Patient data sells higher ($250-1000) — enables ID theft + insurance fraud, stays undetected longer.
- Legacy/unpatched systems = easy entry.
- Insurance = money to pay ransom.

## 3. Trends
- Double extortion now standard — 73% steal data before encrypting. Backup restore ≠ safe.
- Ransom demands doubled 2022→2024 ($1.2M→$2.5M). Total cost ~$2.7M+ per incident.

## 4. MedDefense Fit
- RaaS — top threat, exact target profile.
- Nation-state — low, unless research partnership starts.
- Insider (negligent) — high, known gaps exist today.
- Insider (malicious) — medium, no DLP/monitoring to stop it.
- Hacktivist — low, but portal DDoS still possible.
- Unskilled — high, already happened once.
