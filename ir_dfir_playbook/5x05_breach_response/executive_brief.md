
# Executive Brief — Patient Information Security Incident

**Incident:** IR-2026-0420-01  
**Prepared for:** MedDefense Board of Directors  
**Presented by:** Dr. Patricia Morales, Chief Information Security Officer  
**Reporting period:** Sunday, 20 April 2026, 23:00 UTC  
**Status:** Preliminary — investigation ongoing

## Incident Summary

MedDefense is investigating suspicious activity affecting four clinical workstations and a server containing patient documentation at Main Site.

Staff reported severe workstation slowness and unusual file behavior. The investigation has not established whether patient care was interrupted or whether files were encrypted.

## Patient Information at Risk

The incident scenario reports **1,847 patient-file access events** on April 19, between 02:14 and 02:41 UTC.

The number of individual patients affected has not been independently verified. The investigation has not established whether patient information was transferred outside MedDefense.

## Regulatory Position

The preliminary assessment does not establish a low probability of compromise; Helena Reyes must complete the formal review to determine notification obligations.

## Response Completed

- The incident was formally declared and assigned an initial severity of SEV2.
- The incident response leadership and escalation responsibilities were identified.
- A memory capture from the initial workstation was documented.
- Evidence-preservation priorities were established for the affected systems.
- A preliminary forensic assessment was prepared, with outstanding evidence clearly identified.

## Outstanding Actions

- Verify the preserved evidence and obtain the complete investigation records.
- Establish the number of affected patients and information involved.
- Confirm the extent of unauthorized access and whether information left the organization.
- Verify containment and the safety of affected clinical services.
- Complete the legal assessment and determine notification requirements.
